03 · Agent or automation?

You don't need to know the difference. We do.

In brief, so you know what you're buying.

Automation

Fixed steps

When A happens, do B and C. Reliable, cheap to run, ideal for recurring work that doesn't require decisions.

ExampleA new order gets written to a spreadsheet and a confirmation goes to the customer.

AI agent

Goal & judgement

Gets a goal, not a procedure. Reads, decides, looks things up and chooses the next step on its own. For work that requires thinking.

ExampleReads an inquiry, recognises it's a complaint, looks up the order and prepares a reply.

What we build

Both, with a safety net

Automations where fixed steps are enough. Agents where judgement is needed. And everywhere – checks and human approval where money or the customer is involved.

ResultA system you can trust, because you know when it decides on its own and when it asks you.

10 · Frequently asked

Questions people ask most.

What's the difference between an AI agent and an automation?

An automation follows fixed steps: when an order arrives, write it to a spreadsheet and send a confirmation. An AI agent gets a goal and decides the steps itself: it reads an inquiry, recognises what the customer wants, looks up information and prepares a reply. Most businesses benefit from a combination: automations for fixed steps, agents where judgement is needed.

I'm a freelancer or small business. Is it worth it for me?

Small businesses and freelancers benefit the most – every hour saved is an hour you can invoice. If you spend at least 5 hours a week on routine work, it's worth calculating. If the numbers don't work out, we'll tell you at the audit.

How much does it cost?

The audit is free. Implementation starts at CZK 45,000 for 1–3 automations or agents. Ongoing management from CZK 18,000/month, no long-term commitment. You'll get the exact price in writing after the audit, before committing to anything.

How fast will it work?

The first automation usually runs within 14 days of approving the plan. The whole project typically takes 2–4 weeks.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Everything runs on your accounts and tools, you get documentation and access. If you decide to stop working with us, the system stays yours.

What if AI makes a mistake in front of a customer?

Where money or customer relationships are involved, we build in an approval step: the agent prepares, a human approves with one click. Every agent also has output checks and rules about what it cannot do. We only switch on automatic sending once results have been consistently good.

What happens to our data?

Data stays in your systems. We use tools with business terms that don't use data for model training, and we address personal data processing under GDPR with every client.
