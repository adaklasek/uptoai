Questions you'll have anyway.

We'd rather answer them upfront.

What exactly do we get?

Depends on what you need. Most commonly: customers get answers within 60 minutes instead of a day; reports generate automatically every week; follow-ups go out on their own; your team saves 20–32 hours a week. You'll get an exact list at the audit—free.

How many more customers will I realistically get?

That depends on how many opportunities you're currently losing—which we'll find at the audit. Typically businesses recover deals they were losing due to slow response. One client recovered 3–4 deals per month that would have gone to competitors.

How fast will we see results?

Customer communication and follow-ups—within two weeks. Revenue from better lead capture—within a month. Team time savings—from day one. You'll always know in advance what to expect and when.

What does it cost and when does it pay back?

The audit is free. Implementation from CZK 25,000 (approx. €1,000). Average return on investment within 90 days—through recovered deals, saved hours, or reduced costs. You'll get a specific number after the audit.

Do we need technical expertise?

Not at all. The technical side is our job. You define the outcome you want—we make it work. Your team only learns what they'll actually use every day.

What if we don't see results?

Before we start, we define exactly what success looks like—specific numbers, in writing. If we don't hit them, we keep working without charging more—until it does. We'll never send you another invoice for failing to deliver what we promised.

What will you need from us? Do we have to give you system access?

We need access only to the tools we're directly automating—typically email, CRM, or your messaging platform. You always know exactly what access we have and why. No access to anything unrelated to the project. Access can be revoked any time—and everything we build stays yours.

What happens if we stop working together—will everything still run?

Yes. Everything we set up is yours—no vendor lock-in, no monthly fees to us to keep it running. We hand over a working system with documentation. Your team knows what to do. 30 days of support after handover is included.

Does this work in our industry?

We work with businesses in retail, manufacturing, construction, HR, marketing, consulting, and e-commerce. The industry doesn't matter as much as whether you have repeating processes or customers waiting on responses. If you do, probably yes. If not, we'll tell you honestly at the audit.

Are we too small for AI?

Smaller businesses typically get the most out of it—every hour saved makes a bigger difference than in a large corporation, and every customer you don't lose is visible in revenue. We work with businesses from 5 employees up. Size doesn't matter—results do.
